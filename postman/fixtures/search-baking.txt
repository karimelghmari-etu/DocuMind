Baking bread at home

Mix flour, water, salt and yeast into a dough. Knead the dough for about ten minutes until it is smooth and elastic.
Let the dough rise in a warm place for one hour, until it doubles in size. Shape it into a loaf.
Bake the bread in a preheated oven at 220 degrees for 30 minutes, until the crust is golden and sounds hollow when tapped.
