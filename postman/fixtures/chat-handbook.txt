Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Welcome to Northwind Labs. This handbook explains how we work together, what you can expect from the company, and what the company expects from you. If something is unclear, ask your manager or contact the People team. The handbook is reviewed every year and the latest version is always available on the intranet.

Working hours

Northwind Labs uses flexible working hours. A normal full-time week is 38 hours. You may start your day between 07:30 and 10:00 and organise the rest of your schedule with your team. Everyone is expected to be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00, because most meetings, reviews and customer calls happen in that window. Overtime must be agreed with your manager in advance and is compensated with extra time off, not with money.

Remote work

We believe good work can happen anywhere. Employees may work from home up to three days per week, as long as their role allows it and their team agrees on a shared office day. When working remotely you must use a private, quiet space and follow the same security rules as in the office. Working from another country for more than two weeks requires approval from the People team, because of tax and insurance rules.

Vacation

Every full-time employee receives 25 days of paid vacation each calendar year. Part-time employees receive a proportional amount. Vacation requests must be submitted in the HR tool at least two weeks in advance for periods longer than three days. You may carry over a maximum of five days to the next year; carried-over days must be used before March 31 or they are lost. Public holidays are not counted as vacation days.

Sick leave

If you are ill, inform your manager before 9:30 on the first day of absence, by phone or by message. You do not need to explain the nature of the illness. A doctor's note is required after three consecutive days of absence and must be uploaded in the HR tool. Your salary continues to be paid during sick leave according to the national rules and your contract.

Parental leave

New parents are entitled to support from Northwind Labs. The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid leave, and the secondary caregiver receives six weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can start up to two weeks before the expected birth or adoption date. After returning, parents may work reduced hours during the first three months without a reduction in salary.

Probation and contracts

All new employees start with a probation period of six months. During probation, both sides can end the contract with two weeks of notice. Your manager will hold a check-in meeting after one month and after three months to discuss how things are going. After probation, the notice period of two months applies to both the employee and the company, unless your contract says otherwise.

Performance reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Each review is a conversation between you and your manager about your goals, your results and your development. Salary adjustments are discussed once a year, during the December review. Feedback from colleagues is collected anonymously before each review.

Learning and development

We want everyone to keep learning. Each employee has a training budget of 1,500 euros per employee per year, which can be used for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over. Requests are approved by your manager and booked through the People team. In addition, every employee may spend four hours per month on self-directed learning during working time.

Referrals

Great colleagues often know other great people. If you recommend someone who is hired, you receive a referral bonus of 2,000 euros, paid after the new colleague successfully completes probation. Referrals must be submitted through the careers page before the candidate applies on their own.

Dress code and workplace

There is no formal dress code. Wear what makes you comfortable, but dress appropriately when meeting customers. Keep shared spaces tidy, book meeting rooms in the calendar, and clean the kitchen after use. Pets are welcome in the office on Fridays, as long as everyone on the floor agrees.

Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop, a monitor for home use and a headset. Equipment remains the property of Northwind Labs and must be returned on your last working day. Damage caused by normal use is repaired at no cost to you.
