Northwind Labs Travel and Expense Policy

This policy explains how to plan business trips and how to get work-related costs reimbursed. The goal is to keep travel safe, comfortable and reasonably priced. Costs that are not covered by this policy need written approval from your manager before they are made.

Planning a trip

Business trips must be approved by your manager before anything is booked. Trips with an estimated total cost above 2,000 euros also need approval from the department head. All travel is booked through the company travel portal, which applies our negotiated rates and keeps track of where employees are in case of an emergency. Book at least 14 days before departure whenever possible, because late bookings are usually much more expensive.

Choosing transport

For trips that take less than four hours by train, the train is the preferred option, and you may travel in first class. Flights are booked in economy class. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than six hours, and only when the employee has to work on the day of arrival. Checked baggage is reimbursed when the trip lasts more than three days.

Hotels

Choose a hotel close to the place where you will work. The hotel limit is 180 euros per night in most cities; in London, Paris, New York and Zurich the limit is 250 euros per night. Breakfast is included in this limit when the hotel offers it. Stays with friends or family are allowed, but they are not reimbursed.

Meals

While travelling you receive a daily meal allowance of 60 euros for breakfast, lunch and dinner together. When a meal is already provided, for example at a conference, the allowance is reduced accordingly. Alcoholic drinks are not reimbursed, also not during dinners with customers. Tips are reimbursed up to ten percent of the bill.

Local transport

Use public transport whenever possible. Taxis and ride-hailing services may be used when travelling with heavy equipment, when public transport is not available, or after 22:00. Rental cars need approval in advance and must be the smallest suitable category.

Using your own car

If you use your private car for a business trip, you receive 0.30 euros per kilometre. This amount covers fuel, insurance and wear. Parking fees and tolls are reimbursed separately. Fines for speeding or parking are always paid by the employee.

Company credit card

Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a company credit card. The card may only be used for business expenses. Every payment with the card still needs a receipt and an expense claim, exactly like expenses paid with your own money.

Submitting expenses

Submit your expense claim in the expense tool within 30 days of returning from the trip. Claims submitted later can be rejected. Keep a receipt for every expense above 25 euros; for smaller amounts a short description is enough. If a receipt is lost, fill in the lost receipt form and ask your manager to confirm the expense.

Reimbursement

Approved expenses are paid together with the next monthly salary. If you need an advance for a long or expensive trip, ask the finance team at least one week before departure.

Safety while travelling

Register every trip in the travel portal so that the company can reach you in an emergency. Before travelling to a country with a travel warning, consult the security team. Travel insurance is provided automatically for all business trips.
